About me

A calm, confidential space to be yourself.

I am Sarah Dennis, a psychodynamic counsellor offering a warm, thoughtful and non-judgemental space where you can explore what is happening in your life at your own pace.

Sometimes we can feel stuck, overwhelmed, disconnected or unsure why certain patterns keep repeating. Counselling can offer the time and space to understand yourself more deeply and consider new ways of relating to yourself and others.

I work with a wide range of difficulties and life experiences, and I can offer sessions in Hertford or Stevenage, as well as online and telephone appointments.

The psychodynamic approach

Looking beneath the surface.

Psychodynamic counselling explores how our earlier experiences, relationships and emotions can continue to influence how we think, feel and relate in the present. Together, we can gently explore patterns, feelings and experiences that may be difficult to understand on your own.

01

Understand

Develop a deeper understanding of your thoughts, feelings and patterns.

02

Explore

Gently explore experiences and relationships that may still affect you today.

03

Grow

Build greater self-awareness and create space for meaningful change.
